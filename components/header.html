Shuai Fu is a third-year PhD student at the Australian Institute for Machine Learning (AIML), Adelaide University (AU), advised by Prof. Qi Wu. He is currently working on generative AI, particularly on factual-consistent image generation. Before starting his PhD, he worked as a research assistant at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech) from 2022 to 2024, supervised by Prof. Yu Zhang. His broader interests include machine learning, transfer learning, vision-language learning, robotics, and world modeling.
